## What one percentage point costs
- 0.0 % yearly cost → median +0.0 % final wealth
- 0.5 % yearly cost → median -9.4 % final wealth
- 1.0 % yearly cost → median -17.8 % final wealth
- 2.0 % yearly cost → median -31.5 % final wealth

Reading: one-off purchase costs (commission, stamp duty, even the 0.95 % FX spread) hurt much less than costs that repeat every year on the whole depot (fund fees, frequent trading). The FX spread matters mainly when it is paid again and again by trading.
